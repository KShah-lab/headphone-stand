# My headphone stand
#### i have created a cool but unique under the table headphone stand which is small practical and most importantly portable. Unlike most models and my first version, the new headphone stand works on magnets as it will be attached and removed using a simple magnet. There is also as little contact with the headphones themselves to create a floating effect which also reduces wear and tear for the headphones. it is small and very portable with an overall height of around 15cm. There are stoppers on the under the table section to stop the headphones form sliding off and breaking.
